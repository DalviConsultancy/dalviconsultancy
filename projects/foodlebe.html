About Foodle Be

Foodle Be is an innovative food delivery solution designed to connect customers with their favourite local restaurants. The platform emphasizes real-time visibility and a seamless ordering experience from browse to delivery.

Developed by Dalvi Consultancy with a focus on restaurant operational efficiency. The management dashboard gives restaurant owners full control over menus, orders, and analytics in one place.
