About VPTC

VPTC modernizes public transportation by eliminating the need for physical smart cards. Commuters generate QR codes on their smartphones, scan at entry/exit gates, and pay via UPI or linked cards.

Developed by Dalvi Consultancy as a proof-of-concept for smart city initiatives, VPTC demonstrates how transit systems can go fully digital.

FAQ

What is VPTC?

VPTC is a Virtual Public Transport Card—a digital ticketing system that replaces physical smart cards with QR-based mobile passes.

How does VPTC work?

Users generate QR codes on their mobile device, scan at entry/exit points, and top up balance via UPI or cards. Trip history is tracked automatically.
