Berrybash's website showcases their refreshing fruit-based desserts with vibrant imagery and an easy-to-navigate menu. The platform also supports franchise inquiries for expansion.

Developed by Dalvi Consultancy with emphasis on visual appeal and user engagement. The design uses bright, appetizing colors that match the brand identity.
