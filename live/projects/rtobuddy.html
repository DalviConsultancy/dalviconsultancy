About RTO Buddy

RTO Buddy revolutionizes the traditional RTO experience by digitizing compliance and documentation workflows. From vehicle registration to license renewals, our platform offers a transparent, automated interface that reduces processing time and eliminates manual errors.

Built by Dalvi Consultancy's engineering team in Pune, RTO Buddy serves as the comprehensive digital assistant for agents and vehicle owners navigating transport bureaucracy.
